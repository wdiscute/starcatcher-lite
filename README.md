Starcatcher Lite adds a new fishing mini-game to fishing, and that's it!🎣

***
![Fishing a cod in a river, as one usually does](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/84a97bbca6db96a9d35b81c33aa5917596665d47.png)
***


This mod is meant for people who enjoy Starcatcher's fishing minigame but are not interested in all the fluff. 

**If you enjoy:**

- 100+ new unique fish 
- Collection book 
- Hooks, Bobbers & Baits 
- Aquariums 
- Tackle Boxes 
- Fish Plaques 
- Selling Bin 
- Golden Fishes 
- Size and Weight 
- Fish Descriptions 
- Fishing Tournaments 
- Messages in a Bottle 
- Fishing Hats 
- Trophies 

**Then check out [Starcatcher](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/starcatcher)!**

***
🐟 Compatibility 🐡

Starcatcher Lite has Compatibility with
- Vanilla Fishing
- Aquaculture 2
- Nether Depths Delight
- Any other mod that add fish to vanilla's loot, without adding a new rod
